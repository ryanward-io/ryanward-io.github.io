From 23 to 27 September we drove our BYD Atto 3 from Melbourne to Bright, on to Canberra and back: almost 1,500km, a 50 kWh battery, and two kids under four. Charging took eight stops and totalled $177.32.

The whole trip was better than I could've hoped.

Public chargers ran $0.75 to $0.85 per kWh, apart from the Tesla at Coolac at roughly $0.55. At home it's closer to $0.20.[^costs] Chargers advertise 150 to 300 kW. The Atto 3 tops out at 70 kW and averaged about 50.[^speed]

## Day by day

### Wednesday 23 September: Melbourne to Bright

The first charge was at the Ampol Foodary in Avenel on a 150 kW Evie charger, from 1:28 to 1:56 pm. It delivered 24.57 kWh and cost $19.91.

At 3:49 pm, we plugged in at BP Pulse, Glenrowan North. In 24 minutes it delivered 21.52 kWh for $16.14, leaving the battery at 85%, plenty to get to Bright.

Both spots doubled as breaks: toilets, food, stretched legs, a play area. For neither of these were we here waiting to charge longer than we wanted to be.

We arrived at Bright with about 40% or 20 kWh of charge. In retrospect we probably could've got away with a single stop but it was our first long trip so we played it safe.

### Thursday 24 September: Bright

In Bright we used a 50 kW Evie charger, from 11:25 am to 12:00 pm. It delivered 25.14 kWh for $19.36. I went solo to charge it and grabbed some groceries from IGA and lunch so wasn't actively waiting. Otherwise we walked everywhere in Bright and didn't use the car.

### Friday 25 September: Bright to Canberra

On Friday we made two charges just tapping with card, so I don't have good data for them. First an NRMA station in Holbrook at about 10 am, which cost $29.60, and second the Tesla supercharging station outside Coolac at about 12 pm, which cost $14.59. Disconnecting took some googling; double-unlocking the car did it.

We arrived in Canberra with about 30% and slowly charged back up to 100% using home charging (about 1 to 1.5 kW of power) over the next few days which was free to us (it would've cost our host about $7-$10).

### Saturday 26 September: Canberra

Questacon was fun!

### Sunday 27 September: Canberra to Melbourne

Three charges on the way back.

Sunday meant three stops on the way home: Tarcutta, Wodonga West and Avenel. Avenel was the only annoying one. Four chargers, all busy, so I waited about 15 minutes. We'd arrived on about 10%, so there was no real choice but to chat with others waiting. It was the only time charging held us up, and the kids got out the colouring books and had a good time. We finally got to Melbourne on about 20%.

## Would I do it again?

Yes, this gave me way more confidence for longer trips. If you do regular country trips the Atto 3 probably would get tedious. For us, doing it rarely, it was fine. More chargers would only help though.
