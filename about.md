I was born in the shadow of the Blue Mountains of Sydney before moving further in to the Hills Shire when I was about 4 before moving to Melbourne when I was about 13. Been here ever since and loved it growing up here, although I wouldn't say no to more density and walkability.

Professionally, I've been working for the past decade mostly in property tech data roles. I’m now firmly in data science and loving it, although by next year it may be called AI engineer or something else. Who knows really. I also originally trained as a lawyer and often wonder *what if*, but alas you can't do everything in life and the path I've chosen has been rewarding.

Volunteering has always been there too. For a long time it was with Empowered Together, which focused on sexual assault prevention, although we ultimately closed and merged our work into Sexual Health Victoria - a nice outcome given how far the consent landscape has changed in the past fifteen years. I'm now Secretary (and IT help desk) for my son's kindergarten. This work reminds me that the value we ascribe to work doesn't have to be only in terms of monetary compensation; much of the valuable work done is underpaid or [not paid for at all](https://theconversation.com/unpaid-womens-work-is-worth-427-billion-new-research-shows-see-how-much-your-unpaid-labour-is-worth-267860), especially women's work caring for children at home. 

I've also semi-recently completed some formal postgraduate education in [statistics](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/study/course/Graduate-Certificate-of-Applied-Statistics-GC-APPS/local). I do enjoy formal learning so perhaps a Masters is on the cards. We'll see.
