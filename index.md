# Hi. I'm Ryan Ward

**I'm a data scientist that loves solving problems, programming and going through new data.**

**Currently, I'm experimenting with AI, writing, travelling with family, and raising my two kids.**

I'm a trained lawyer, also hold a degree in Biomedical Science, and have an early career in corporate strategy. With this unusual background, I approach data and analytics with a unique perspective focused on the good it can do.

My personal ethics guide what I choose to work on. My work should:

- help explain information, not spread misinformation;
- be respectful and consentful, not abusing trust;
- mitigate bias and amplify missing perspectives, not propagate the status quo.

I have been in almost every area – data, research, strategy, business, product, operations, engineering.

I also co-founded and was COO for 8 years of Empowered Together, a charity that educated students on sexual assault and consent.

This blog is mostly a collection of my thoughts for me to come back to, and a place for me to practise and showcase personal projects. If anything here helps you too, all the better!
